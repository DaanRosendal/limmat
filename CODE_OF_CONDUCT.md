# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small project, and the rules are short.

- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree with the code, not the person.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination, in issues, pull requests, or anywhere else people meet around the project.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep reports and reviews specific: say what you saw and what you expected.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, email contact@daanrosendal.com.
